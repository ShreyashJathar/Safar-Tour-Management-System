**Safar** is a modern, feature-rich luxury travel booking platform built with **React**, **Vite**, **Express**, and **MySQL**. It offers an immersive travel experience including tour packages, luxury hotel reservations, interactive destination maps, weather widgets, AI travel assistance, and an administrative panel for full platform management.
---
## ✨ Features
### 🧳 Traveler Experience
- **Tour & Hotel Discovery**: Browse luxury tours and top-rated hotels with category filters (Nature, Adventure, Cultural, Beach, Luxury) and price sliders.
- **Interactive Map Explorer**: View destination maps, points of interest, and location previews.
- **Real-Time Weather Widget**: Dynamic weather forecasts and seasonal recommendations for destinations.
- **Multi-Step Booking Wizard**: Seamless multi-step booking process with date pickers, traveler count, add-on selections, price breakdown, and checkout.
- **Safar AI Copilot**: Built-in AI travel assistant providing personalized recommendations, itinerary planning, and destination insights.
- **User Dashboard**: Manage upcoming & completed bookings, cancel reservations, and curate a personal wishlist.
- **Community Stories**: Read and share travel experiences, ratings, and guest testimonials.
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Elegant theme switcher featuring glassmorphic designs and custom color palettes.
