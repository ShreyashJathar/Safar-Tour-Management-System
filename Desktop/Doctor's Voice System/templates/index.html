Available Voice Commands

Navigation

  • "go to dashboard" or "dashboard"
  • "register patient" or "go to register"
  • "new prescription" or "go to prescription"
  • "patient records" or "go to records"
  • "go to reports" or "analytics"
  • "logout"

Patient Registration

  • Use Quick Voice Fill and speak: "Name Alex Green Age 30 Gender Male Contact 9876543210" to fill the entire form.
  • Alternatively, dictate individual fields by clicking their microphone icon.

Prescription Writing

  • Dictate symptoms, diagnosis, and advice in their respective fields using field mic buttons.
  • Use Voice Add Medicine and say: "Medicine Aspirin Dosage once daily Duration ten days" to add it.
  • Say "generate prescription" to save and create the PDF prescription.
  • Say "clear form" to start over.

Records & Audio playback

  • Say "search [Patient Name]" (e.g. "search Emily") to lookup details instantly.
  • Say "read prescription" or "read details" to hear patient information/prescriptions spoken aloud.
